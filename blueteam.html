Find & Fix Exercise

Mission briefing

You'll get 6 tickets, one at a time, each describing something that's just been reported. For each one, pick the correct action. Some "obvious" answers are traps — a real analyst thinks about what actually fixes the problem, not just what feels cautious.

There's a timer running for atmosphere — it doesn't lock you out, so take the time you need to think.

All tickets, names, and systems shown are fictional and for training purposes only.
